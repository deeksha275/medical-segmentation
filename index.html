📋 About Medical Image Segmentation

Medical image segmentation is a critical task in medical imaging that involves identifying and delineating specific structures, organs, or abnormalities within medical scans such as MRI, CT, or X-ray images. This process is essential for diagnosis, treatment planning, and monitoring disease progression.

Using advanced deep learning techniques like U-NET architecture, we can automatically segment anatomical structures with high precision, assisting radiologists and clinicians in making faster and more accurate diagnoses.

🏗️ U-NET Architecture

U-NET is a convolutional neural network architecture designed specifically for biomedical image segmentation. It consists of a contracting path (encoder) that captures context and a symmetric expanding path (decoder) that enables precise localization. The skip connections between encoder and decoder help preserve spatial information lost during downsampling.

🎯 High Accuracy

U-NET achieves state-of-the-art segmentation accuracy on medical imaging datasets, often matching or exceeding human expert performance.

🏥 Clinical Applications

Tumor Detection & Segmentation: Identifying and delineating brain tumors, lung nodules, liver lesions, and other abnormalities.

Organ Segmentation: Segmenting organs such as heart, kidneys, liver, lungs for volume measurement and surgical planning.

Vascular Analysis: Segmenting blood vessels for studying vascular diseases and planning interventions.
